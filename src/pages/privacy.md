TripEleven ("TripEleven," "we," "us," or "our") provides a platform where tour, trekking and travel businesses build trip catalogs, publish websites, collect inquiries and bookings, connect payment processors and manage content. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.

It applies to our website (tripeleven.com), the dashboard (app.tripeleven.com), websites published through the platform, and our related services (together, the "Platform"). It forms part of, and should be read with, our [Terms & Conditions](/terms). Terms such as "Customer," "Visitors," "Customer Content" and "Plan" have the same meaning as in those Terms.

## 1. Our role: controller and processor

We deal with two groups of people, and our role differs for each.

**Customers** are the businesses and individuals who hold a TripEleven account, and the staff members they invite. For account, billing and Platform usage data, TripEleven is the data controller: we decide why and how that data is used.

**Visitors** are people who browse a Customer's published website, send an inquiry or make a booking. For this data, the Customer is the data controller and TripEleven is a processor, handling it only on the Customer's behalf to run the Platform. Visitors should read the privacy notice of the business they are dealing with and send requests about their data to that business first.

This policy does not cover the practices of Customers, Stripe or other third parties, who have their own policies.

## 2. Information we collect

### From Customers and their staff

- **Account details:** name, email address, business name, phone number (if provided), role, and password (stored in hashed form, not plain text).
- **Billing details:** selected Plan, billing history and billing contact. A payment method is required only when you subscribe to a paid Plan. Card details are collected and processed by Stripe, and we do not store full card numbers.
- **Customer Content:** trips, itineraries, prices, photos, blog posts, branding, domain and DNS settings, and inquiry replies.
- **Staff member details:** name, email and permissions of people you invite to your account.
- Support messages and anything else you send us.

### From Visitors on a Customer's website

- Inquiry and booking details submitted through forms, such as name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, travel dates, group size and message content.
- Payment-related information handled by the Customer's connected payment account (for example Stripe). We do not hold Visitors' card numbers.
- Technical data such as IP address, browser type, device, pages viewed and referring page.

### Collected automatically from everyone

- **Usage data:** pages viewed, features used and interaction patterns.
- **Device and log data:** IP address, browser, operating system, language, timestamps and error logs.
- **Cookies and similar technologies** (see Section 9).

### From third parties

- Payment status and account confirmations from Stripe.
- Messages passing through channels a Customer enables, such as WhatsApp or email.

## 4. Free trials and payment information

To start a 7-day free trial, you do not need to provide a credit card or any payment method, and you are not charged during the trial. When the trial ends, access continues only if you subscribe to a paid Plan. At that point your payment method is collected and processed by Stripe, and we receive from Stripe only the information needed to manage your subscription, such as payment status, card type and the last four digits.

## 5. AI features

Where the Platform offers AI-assisted features, such as itinerary drafting, the text you submit (for example trip details and prompts) is sent to the AI provider that powers the feature and is handled under that provider's terms and privacy policy. If you connect your own AI provider account or key, your usage is also subject to that provider's terms. Do not include Visitors' personal data in AI prompts. AI output is a drafting aid only, and you are responsible for checking it before use (see our [Terms](/terms)).

## 6. Who we share data with

We share personal data only as follows:

- **Customers:** Visitor inquiry and booking data goes to the Customer the Visitor contacted.
- **Service providers (processors):** companies that help us run the Platform, such as cloud hosting and storage, payment processing (Stripe), email delivery, analytics, error monitoring, live chat (Tawk.to) and AI providers. They may use data only to provide services to us and must protect it.
- **Third-party services a Customer enables,** such as WhatsApp or other connected payment processors.
- **Legal and safety reasons:** where required by law, court order or a valid government request, or to protect the rights, safety and security of TripEleven, our users or the public.
- **Business transfers:** if we are involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, data may be transferred. We will notify you if your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.
- With your consent or at your direction.

## 7. International transfers

TripEleven is based in Nepal, and our providers may process data in other countries, where data protection laws can differ. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards, such as contractual protections, for these transfers.

## 9. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use these categories:

| Category            | Purpose                                                                                                                                        | Can you turn it off?                                             |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Essential           | Login, session management, security and basic settings                                                                                         | No, the Platform will not work without them                      |
| Analytics           | Understand how people use the Platform so we can improve it                                                                                    | Yes                                                              |
| Live chat (Tawk.to) | Provide chat support and keep an ongoing chat running. Tawk.to may set its own cookies and collect technical data under its own privacy policy | Yes, through our cookie settings (where offered) or your browser |

**Your choices.** You can accept or reject non-essential cookies through our cookie banner (where shown) or your browser settings, and delete existing cookies at any time. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the Platform from working.

**Customers' websites.** Websites published through TripEleven may use additional cookies or tools chosen by the Customer, who is responsible for explaining them to their Visitors.

## 10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, password hashing and trusted providers such as Stripe for payments. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Use a strong, unique password and keep your login details private, and tell us promptly at [hello@tripeleven.com](mailto:hello@tripeleven.com) if you suspect unauthorized access. If a data breach affects you, we will notify you and any required authorities as the law requires.

## 11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you.
- Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
- Delete your data.
- Restrict or object to certain uses.
- Receive your data in a portable format.
- Withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent.
- Complain to your local data protection authority.

To use these rights, email [hello@tripeleven.com](mailto:hello@tripeleven.com). We may need to verify your identity first and will respond within a reasonable time, and within [30] days where the law sets a deadline. If your request concerns data a Visitor gave to a Customer (for example, a trip inquiry), we may refer you to that Customer, since they control that data.

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in the email. Service messages, such as security and billing notices, will still be sent.

## 12. Children

The Platform is for businesses and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through account sign-ups. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it. Customers are responsible for handling any traveler data about minors in line with the law.
